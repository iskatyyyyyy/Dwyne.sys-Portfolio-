# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: employers and hiring managers reviewing the site to judge skill and fit. Success means they quickly understand what was built, why, and what it says about the owner, and can reach the owner without friction. The contact method is undecided.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that presents the owner's engineering projects with enough clarity and polish that a hiring manager can grasp the work fast and reach out. The site is currently being initialized; no final content exists yet.

## Positioning

Undecided. The one-line claim about who the owner is and what their work stands for has not been set and was explicitly deferred.

## Operating Context

- The owner is currently organizing past projects and plans to use Google Stitch to bring a cohesive visual vision to life before building out the site.
- This folder is being initialized now; everything in it is a placeholder until real content is gathered.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, Tailwind CSS 4, TypeScript.
- The current `app/page.tsx` content (a placeholder grid referencing "Iluvatar — checkers match simulation" and "edge computing Raspberry Pi snail detector") is explicitly placeholder and must not be treated as final copy.
- The surface must accommodate a growing, curated project list as projects are organized.
- Undecided: real project list, per-project copy and imagery, contact method, deployed URL, and identity details (name, title, one-line description).

## Brand Commitments

None confirmed. Name, title, and the public one-line description are explicitly deferred by the owner.

## Evidence on Hand

No real portfolio content yet. The only project references are placeholder comments in `app/page.tsx`. Future work must not fabricate testimonials, metrics, customers, or deployment claims.

## Product Principles

- Let the work speak: projects are the core artifact; everything else supports them.
- Hiring-manager-first: a visitor should grasp skills fast and reach the owner without friction.
- Grow with the owner: the site is structured to add real projects as they are organized.
- Truth over filler: only real, organized projects appear; placeholders never ship as final copy.
